# Arcane Crafting Terminal

**Summary (for the Modrinth summary field, 138 characters):**

> A Thaumaturge addon for Tom's Simple Storage: a Crafting Terminal with a built-in Arcane Workbench that crafts straight from your storage.

---

## Arcane crafting, straight from your storage

Tired of running between chests to gather ingredients, crystals and a wand before every arcane craft? The **Arcane Crafting Terminal** puts a full Arcane Workbench inside a Tom's Simple Storage terminal. You can browse, search and pull items from your whole storage network and craft arcane recipes in the same screen.

This is an addon for **[Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024)** and **[Tom's Simple Storage](https://modrinth.com/mod/toms-storage)**.

## Features

- **Everything an Arcane Workbench has.** A 3×3 crafting grid, a slot for each of the six primal crystals, and a wand slot. The vis cost, available aura, crystal glows and wand payment are shown just like on Thaumaturge's own workbench.
- **Everything a Crafting Terminal has.** Search, sorting, scrolling and tall mode, the side buttons, and a clear-grid button that sends the grid back to storage.
- **Arcane and normal recipes.** Arcane recipes are paid for the Thaumaturge way: wand vis, crystals and aura. Regular crafting recipes work too.
- **Automatic refilling.** When a craft uses up an ingredient, the terminal refills that slot from your storage, so shift-clicking the result keeps crafting.
- **Two layouts.** One button switches between the Arcane Workbench layout and the classic Crafting Terminal layout. Each terminal remembers its layout, and your crystals and wand stay put while their slots are hidden.
- **Charger support.** Put an Arcane Workbench Charger on top of your network's **Inventory Connector**. Every Arcane Crafting Terminal on that network then draws vis from the 3×3 chunks around the connector.
- **Wireless access.** Tom's Advanced Wireless Terminal can be bound to an Arcane Crafting Terminal, so you can use it from afar.
- **Thaumonomicon entry.** The terminal has its own research page in the Basics tab, right next to First Steps.
- **Thaumometer aspects.** You can scan the Arcane Crafting Terminal and Tom's Advanced Wireless Terminal with the Thaumometer.
- **JEI support.** The terminal is listed as a station for arcane and crafting recipes. The "+" button fills the grid and the crystal slots from your storage network and your inventory.
- **Translations:** English and Ukrainian.

## How to get one

1. Complete the **Arcane Crafting Terminal** research in the Thaumonomicon (Basics tab).
2. Build a normal **Crafting Terminal** from Tom's Simple Storage and place it on an inventory or on your storage network.
3. Use **Salis Mundus** on it. After a moment it turns into an Arcane Crafting Terminal. It keeps its facing and its connection to your storage.

To craft with more aura, place an **Arcane Workbench Charger** on the network's **Inventory Connector**.

## Notes

- The Arcane Workbench layout is taller than the Crafting Terminal layout. If it doesn't fit your screen, lower your GUI scale or switch to the Crafting Terminal layout.
